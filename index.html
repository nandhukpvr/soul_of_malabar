01 THE TRADITION

MORE THAN A PERFORMANCE

A journey into living tradition.

Theyyam is a centuries-old ritual art form of North Malabar, Kerala, that blends worship, folklore, music, dance and vibrant visual traditions. It is primarily performed in districts such as Kannur and Kasaragod, often in sacred groves, village shrines and traditional temples. Each Theyyam represents a deity, ancestral spirit or legendary figure, brought to life through elaborate costumes, striking face painting and powerful movements. Accompanied by traditional percussion, the performance creates a deeply spiritual atmosphere for devotees and visitors alike. Passed down through generations, Theyyam preserves local legends, community beliefs and the cultural heritage of the region. More than a performance, Theyyam is a living tradition in which the divine is believed to manifest through the performer.

From the first rhythm of the chenda to the transformation of the performer, every moment carries a story. Our journeys are designed to help visitors understand the people, places, rituals and traditions surrounding each Theyyam experience.

08

Kathivannur Veeran

Kathivannur Veeran was a legendary warrior of North Malabar, remembered for his exceptional courage, martial skill and devotion to those he loved. His story of bravery, love and sacrifice lives on through the powerful Kathivannur Veeran Theyyam.

09

Gulikan

Gulikan is a fierce Theyyam traditionally associated with Lord Shiva and the divine power over death, destiny and protection. Known for his striking appearance and intense movements, Gulikan brings a powerful sense of mystery and spirituality to the ritual traditions of North Malabar.

10

Pottan Theyyam

A significant Theyyam associated with social themes, equality and the questioning of caste-based distinctions.

11

Kuttichathan

Kuttichathan is a mischievous and powerful Theyyam associated with Lord Shiva and revered in many parts of North Malabar. Known for its distinctive rituals, energetic movements and playful yet mysterious character.

12

Panchuruli

powerful forest-associated ritual form, known for its striking appearance, energetic movements and deep connection with the wild spirit of Kasargod and Mangalore regions.

13

Kadavankott Maakkam

Tragic tale of a courageous woman whose story reflects betrayal, injustice, and the enduring power of a mother's love. Makkam preserves a legend of suffering, sacrifice, and the quest for justice through ritual and storytelling.

05 BEHIND THE RITUAL

Stories behind the face.

THE TRANSFORMATION

Before the drums begin.

Long before the drums begin, Theyyam comes alive through hours of preparation, devotion, and tradition. Elaborate costumes, intricate face painting, towering headgear, and sacred ornaments are carefully prepared, each carrying its own cultural significance. Traditional music, prayers, and rituals create an atmosphere of anticipation as the performer prepares to embody the divine.

For visitors, these moments offer a glimpse into the spiritual world behind the spectacle. Our local guides help you understand the stories, symbolism, and customs of each performance while encouraging respectful observation, mindful photography, and sensitivity towards the sacred spaces and communities that keep this ancient heritage alive.

09 TRAVEL WITH RESPECT

Witness. Don't disturb.

Theyyam is a sacred ritual deeply rooted in the faith, traditions, and cultural heritage of North Malabar, rather than a conventional stage performance. We encourage every visitor to approach each ritual with curiosity, humility, and respect for the beliefs of the local community. Our guides help you understand the customs, follow local instructions, and observe performances without disturbing the sacred atmosphere. Photography and filming should always follow the rules of each venue, ensuring that the dignity of the ritual and the privacy of devotees remain respected.
